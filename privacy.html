Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

AI Learning Path ("the app", "we") turns a document you provide into an interactive course. This page explains what it collects to do that, and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Account info. Your email address and password, handled entirely by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) — the app itself never sees or stores your password.
  • What you upload. The PDF or text you provide to build a course, and the course, lessons, and quiz answers generated from it.
  • Your progress. Which lessons you've completed, quiz results, and streak/usage counts, so the app can track what you've learned and when to review it.
  • Local device settings. Theme preference and which course you last opened are kept in your browser's local storage, not sent to us.

We don't use cookies, ads, or any analytics or tracking service. Nothing here is sold or shared for advertising.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in. Your account data and uploaded material are stored on Supabase's infrastructure in [HOSTING REGION, e.g. the EU].
  • Anthropic processes excerpts of your document — sent from our server, not directly from your browser — to generate the course, lessons, and quiz questions. Anthropic acts as a data processor for this purpose; see Anthropic's privacy policy.

We don't share your material or account with anyone else.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. You can delete an individual course at any time, or your entire account — including every course, all progress, and the account itself — from Account settings. Account deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Your rights

You can access everything the app holds about you by opening it and looking at your library and account screen — nothing is hidden. You can delete any of it yourself at any time, without contacting anyone, from the app's Account screen. If you're in a jurisdiction that grants further rights over your data (such as the EU's GDPR or California's CCPA) and self-service deletion doesn't cover something you need, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Security

Every table is access-controlled so an account can only ever read or write its own rows. Traffic to and from the app is encrypted (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but nothing here is stored or transmitted in plain text where a standard protection exists to avoid it.

Children

This app is not directed at children under [13 or 16 — pick per your jurisdiction], and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.
