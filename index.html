Questions people ask first

What do I actually get from one upload?

A course: a path of short lessons built from your document, each one explaining a single idea and ending in a question that checks you got it. How many lessons depends on your plan — between 10 and 15 per course — and the app schedules reviews so ideas come back before they fade rather than all at once the night before.

What kinds of files can I upload?

PDF or plain text, up to roughly 50 pages. You can also paste text straight in if what you want to study isn't in a file.

Scanned PDFs work too — the text is read off the page images by software running inside your own browser, in Hebrew and English. The images never leave your device. For a long book, the tools/pdf_prep script in this project prepares a bundle the app reads whole.

What happens to my document?

It's stored on your account so your course keeps working, on Supabase's infrastructure in the EU. Excerpts of it are sent from our server to Anthropic's Claude models to write the lessons — excerpts of your own material, never the whole document, and never directly from your browser.

There are no cookies, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising. The Privacy Policy lists every service that touches your data and why.

Is it free to try?

Yes. A new account gets a free trial course straight away, with no card. You only choose a plan if you want to keep building courses after it.

How do payment and cancelling work?

Paying opens Grow's own secure payment page — your card details go to Grow and never reach us. Your plan updates within a few seconds of paying.

Cancel any time from Account → Manage billing. You keep the plan until the period you've already paid for runs out, and everything you've built keeps working either way: you can open any course, replay any lesson, and run reviews as often as you like without it counting against a limit.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, yourself, without emailing anyone. Delete a single course from your library, or your whole account — every course, all progress, and the account itself — from the Account screen. Deletion is immediate and can't be undone.

Still have a question?

We answer within one business day. Email mayangabinet@gmail.com — a real person reads it. If it's about a charge, include the email address you paid with so it can be found without a back-and-forth.
