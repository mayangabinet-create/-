Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

AI Learning Path ("the app", "we") turns a document you provide into an interactive course. This page explains what it collects to do that, and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Account info. Your email address and password, handled entirely by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) — the app itself never sees or stores your password.
  • What you upload. The PDF or text you provide to build a course, and the course, lessons, and quiz answers generated from it. If your PDF is a scan, the text is read off the page images by software running inside your own browser — the images themselves are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Billing info, if you subscribe. Your card details go directly to Grow (Meshulam) and never reach our servers. What we keep on our side is a token Grow gives us that represents your saved card for renewing your subscription automatically — not the card number itself — plus your plan's status and renewal date.
  • Your progress. Which lessons you've completed, quiz results, and streak/usage counts, so the app can track what you've learned and when to review it.
  • Local device settings. Theme preference and which course you last opened are kept in your browser's local storage, not sent to us.

We don't use cookies, ads, or any analytics or tracking service. Nothing here is sold or shared for advertising.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in. Your account data and uploaded material are stored on Supabase's infrastructure in the EU (Paris, France).
  • Anthropic processes excerpts of your document — sent from our server, not directly from your browser — to generate the course, lessons, and quiz questions. Anthropic acts as a data processor for this purpose; see Anthropic's privacy policy. The models we send it to are Anthropic's Claude family — currently Haiku, Sonnet and Opus, which one depending on your plan and on whether the call is planning a course or writing a lesson. We change which model handles which job as they improve; it is always Anthropic, and it is always excerpts of your own material rather than the whole document.
  • Grow (Meshulam) processes payment if you subscribe to a paid plan — your card details, billing address, and payment history are Grow's, not ours. Each month, our systems request that Grow charge your saved card again for the renewal, using the token described above; we only ever see your subscription's status and renewal date, never the charge happening. See Grow's website for their own privacy policy.

We don't share your material or account with anyone else.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. You can delete an individual course at any time, or your entire account — including every course, all progress, and the account itself — from Account settings. Account deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you've subscribed, cancel first from Account → Manage billing — deleting the account while a subscription is active is blocked until you do. Once deleted, your saved card token is deleted with it, so nothing here can charge you again; Grow keeps its own records of past payments independently of us, for as long as it's required to for accounting and legal reasons.

Your rights

You can access everything the app holds about you by opening it and looking at your library and account screen — nothing is hidden. You can delete any of it yourself at any time, without contacting anyone, from the app's Account screen. Israel's Protection of Privacy Law gives you the right to inspect and correct data held about you; if you're in a jurisdiction that grants further rights (such as the EU's GDPR or California's CCPA) and self-service deletion doesn't cover something you need, contact us at mayangabinet@gmail.com.

Security

Every table is access-controlled so an account can only ever read or write its own rows. Traffic to and from the app is encrypted (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but nothing here is stored or transmitted in plain text where a standard protection exists to avoid it.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the update.
